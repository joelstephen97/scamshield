0.14.1 adds no permission, no host and no network request; nothing is sent anywhere. The only hosts contacted are unchanged: raw.githubusercontent.com (public threat feed and hourly hot list) and the opt-in community-report relay. It is a bug-fix release: (1) the protection on/off switch now also pauses declarativeNetRequest blocking, not only page warnings, and is no longer synced between devices; (2) "Trust this site", pause and mute on shared-hosting platforms (e.g. pages.dev, vercel.app) now apply to that single subdomain only, and allow rules never cover a whole platform; (3) the hourly hot list is retained for up to 48 hours past expiry; a malformed list, one naming well-known safe sites, or one that suddenly grows several times larger is rejected or held back before it can block anything, and free code-hosting sites (github.io, gitlab.io) are now covered by the hourly list; (4) a cleared feed URL stays cleared and then no feed or hot-list download is made; (5) steadier update timers and feed jobs; the crypto-wallet confirmation no longer approves on its own. Warning labels and wording are updated in all 20 languages. To test quickly: turn the switch off in the popup and open a blocked test URL (it loads; switch on and it is blocked); trust a site on a shared-hosting platform and confirm a sibling subdomain is still checked. No remote code, no WebAssembly; all detection is unminified vanilla JS bundled in the package. Data collection permissions unchanged: required none, optional websiteActivity for the off-by-default community reporting. Source: https://github.com/joelstephen97/scamshield (tag v0.14.1).
