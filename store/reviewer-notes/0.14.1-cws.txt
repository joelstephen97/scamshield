No new permissions since 0.3.1 (still storage, declarativeNetRequest, alarms + http/https), no new hosts, no new network requests, nothing sent anywhere. The only hosts contacted are unchanged: raw.githubusercontent.com (the public threat feed and hourly hot list in the scamshield-feed repository) and the opt-in community-report relay. 0.14.1 is a bug-fix release: (1) the protection on/off switch now also pauses declarativeNetRequest blocking (previously it only paused page warnings) and is no longer synced between devices; (2) "Trust this site", pause and mute on shared-hosting platforms (e.g. pages.dev, vercel.app) now scope to that single subdomain, and network allow rules never cover a whole platform; (3) the hourly hot list is kept for up to 48 hours past expiry, and a malformed list, one naming well-known safe sites, or one that suddenly grows several times larger is rejected or held back before it can block anything; scam pages on free code-hosting sites (github.io, gitlab.io) are now covered by the hourly list; (4) a cleared feed URL stays cleared, and with it empty no feed or hot-list download is made; (5) update timers and feed jobs are steadier, and the crypto-wallet confirmation no longer approves on its own. Warning labels and wording are updated in all 20 languages. CWS data-use form answers unchanged. How to test quickly: turn the switch off in the popup and open a known-blocked test URL (it should load; turn it on and it is blocked); trust a site on a shared-hosting platform and confirm a sibling subdomain is still checked. Source: github.com/joelstephen97/scamshield, tag v0.14.1.
