No new permissions since 0.3.1 (still storage, declarativeNetRequest, alarms + http/https), no new hosts, no new network requests, nothing sent anywhere. The only hosts contacted are unchanged: raw.githubusercontent.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net (the public threat feed and hourly hot list in the scamshield-feed repository), the visited page's own favicon, and the off-by-default opt-in community-report relay. 0.15.0 is a precision and UX release: (1) far fewer false warnings (popular and regional sites, Google country domains, bot-check pages and logged-in feeds no longer flagged; at most one warning per site per day; a "This site is fine" button); (2) warnings are rendered in an isolated shadow root so page styles cannot break them, and fit phone to ultra-wide, light/dark/RTL; (3) links to listed scam sites are marked as the user scrolls and ask before opening, scam ads from listed hosts are hidden (not a general ad blocker), and declarativeNetRequest now also blocks sub-frames and scripts from listed hosts; (4) a page script that tries to send a password the user typed to a Telegram bot or Discord webhook is blocked locally (api.telegram.org / discord.com appear only as patterns matched in page requests; the extension never contacts them); (5) Options gains a local "What ScamShield showed" log with Copy diagnostics (clipboard only, never uploaded); (6) Firefox for Android support. Strings updated in all 20 languages. CWS data-use form answers unchanged. How to test quickly: open http://scamshield-test-blocked.example/ (a test sentinel in the bundled ruleset) and it is blocked; browse a few popular sites and nothing appears; open Options -> What ScamShield showed. Source: github.com/joelstephen97/scamshield, tag v0.15.0.
