Protection

Everything here runs on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on community reporting at the bottom.

Protection onScans pages for scams and warns you. Turn off to pause everything.
Scams & phishingBlocking known-bad sites and catching new phishing on your device.
Block known scam sitesStops pages on the daily-updated threat list before they load.
Page analysisReads the page's wording, layout and icons on your device to catch scams that aren't on any list yet.
Fake-CAPTCHA / "paste this" blockerStops pages that tell you to press Win+R and paste a command (ClickFix malware).
Fake browser-update blockerBlocks pages pretending your browser needs an update so you download malware.
Hide scam contentGreys out "you've won a prize" blocks inside otherwise normal pages.

ShoppingSpots fake online shops and where search ads really go.
Fake-shop checksLooks for fake countdowns, fake "only 2 left" pressure, missing contact details and off-platform payment (Zelle/wire/crypto), and shows them in the popup.
Sponsored-result checkOn Google/Bing/DuckDuckGo, flags a sponsored result whose ad goes somewhere other than the site it shows.
Crypto & walletsWarnings before risky wallet signatures.
Wallet drainer protectionWarns before you approve token spends, "Permit" signatures or account delegations that drainers abuse.
PrivacyCatches sites that leak your details or track your device — all on your device.
Leaky-form warningWarns when a site sends the email or phone you typed to a tracker before you press submit.
Fingerprinting detectionTells you which script is fingerprinting your device to track you across sites.
Notification-trap warningFlags the "click Allow to continue" trick sites use to spam you with pop-ups.
Strict modeFor anyone who wants maximum caution: even "suspicious" pages are blocked with a full-screen warning and simpler wording. Best for helping a less confident family member stay safe.
Help make ScamShield smarterSends the site name (never the full address or page text) and anonymous risk signals of pages flagged dangerous, and of sites you report. Off by default.
A report contains only: the site name (hostname), the verdict and internal reason codes, numeric risk signals derived from the address and page layout (hashed word counts and counts of forms, inputs and links — never the text itself), matched brand names, the extension version, and the hour. Never the full address (path or query), page text, anything you typed, cookies, or any identifier of you or your device. Reports are deleted after 180 days. Privacy policy

        What leaves your device

        ScamShield makes no analytics, telemetry or tracking requests — ever. The only requests it can make are these three, and you can verify it yourself: open your browser's DevTools → Network tab, use ScamShield normally, and you will see nothing beyond them.

        • Threat-list download
          The public blocklist file — the same for everyone, nothing about you is sent.          —
        • Community reports
          Only if you turned reporting on. Site name + numeric signals, never URLs or page text.          —
        • Page icon check
          Fetches a page's own favicon to spot brand look-alikes. No third party, no cookies.          per page
