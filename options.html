Protection

Everything here runs on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on the last option.

Protection onScans pages for scams and warns you. Turn off to pause everything.
Block known scam sitesStops pages on the daily-updated threat list before they load.
Hide scam contentGreys out "you've won a prize" blocks inside otherwise normal pages.
Page analysisReads the page's wording, layout and icons on your device to catch scams that aren't on any list yet.
Help make ScamShield smarterSends the site name (never the full address or page text) and anonymous risk signals of pages flagged dangerous, and of sites you report. Off by default.
A report contains only: the site name (hostname), the verdict and internal reason codes, numeric risk signals derived from the address and page layout (hashed word counts and counts of forms, inputs and links — never the text itself), matched brand names, the extension version, and the hour. Never the full address (path or query), page text, anything you typed, cookies, or any identifier of you or your device. Reports are deleted after 180 days. Privacy policy
