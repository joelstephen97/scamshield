Why it needs access to your sites

Your browser words this permission as “read and change all your data on all websites”. ScamShield needs it to look at the page you are on and warn you before you type. Every check runs on your device, and nothing leaves your browser unless you choose to share a report in Settings.

Pin ScamShield

Click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar, then the pin next to ScamShield, so you can see each page's status at a glance.

Open settings

ScamShield is free. Optional: in Settings you can help make it smarter by sharing anonymous reports of scams it catches — it's off unless you turn it on. If it helps you, you can support development from the Settings page.
