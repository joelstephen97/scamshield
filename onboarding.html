Parry is protecting you

It watches the pages you visit and warns you about scams — entirely on your device. Your browsing never leaves your computer.

Phishing & fake logins

Warns when a page sends your password to the wrong site.

Brand look-alikes

Recognises a copied PayPal, bank or telco icon on a site that isn't theirs — 60+ brands, including UAE banks and government services.

Page analysis

Reads the wording and layout of login pages on your device to catch scams not yet on any list.

Crypto wallet guard

Stops drainer approvals and recovery-phrase theft before your wallet sees them.

Clipboard & "paste this" scams

Alerts you when a site secretly copies a command to your clipboard.

Fake virus pop-ups

Breaks the scary pop-up loop and helps you leave.

Pin Parry

Click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar, then the pin next to Parry, so you can see each page's status at a glance.

Open settings

Parry is free. Optional: in Settings you can help make it smarter by sharing anonymous reports of scams it catches — it's off unless you turn it on. If it helps you, you can support development from the Settings page.
